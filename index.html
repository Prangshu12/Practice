Understand Assam

Know the Natural Disasters Around Us

Assam's geography, rivers, hills and monsoon climate create different kinds of natural hazards. Understanding them is the first step toward preparedness.

🌊

Flood

Prepare for rising water, evacuation, safe drinking water and protecting documents.

🏚️

Earthquake

Learn Drop, Cover and Hold On, safe exits and earthquake-resistant practices.

⛰️

Landslide

Recognize warning signs around slopes, roads and vulnerable hill areas.

🌪️

Storm

Secure homes, follow warnings and stay away from dangerous structures.

🏞️

River Erosion

Understand changing riverbanks and community-based adaptation strategies.

Future Ready Assam

Smart Solutions for Disaster Resilience

Technology, local knowledge and community participation can work together to reduce disaster risks.

📡

Smart Early Warning

River-level sensors, rainfall monitoring, automated alerts and community warning systems can provide earlier information.

🤖

AI & Data

Data analysis can help identify vulnerable locations and improve disaster preparedness planning.

📱

Offline Communication

Community radio, mesh networks and offline communication plans can support people when conventional networks fail.

🌳

Nature-Based Solutions

Wetlands, forests, vegetation and healthy river ecosystems can contribute to long-term resilience.

🏗️

Resilient Buildings

Safer construction practices and appropriate structural improvements can reduce risk.

🗺️

Digital Community Maps

Communities can map shelters, vulnerable areas, safe routes, water points and important services.
